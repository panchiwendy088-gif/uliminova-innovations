About the company

Practical innovation for a stronger agricultural future.

Company introduction

ulimiNova Innovations is an agricultural technology company focused on improving farming through modern, practical and data-driven solutions. The company helps farmers monitor crops, manage irrigation and make better decisions using accessible technology.

Vision

To become a leading agricultural innovation company that uses technology and data to create productive, sustainable and resilient farming communities.

Mission

To provide practical and affordable agricultural technologies that help farmers improve productivity, manage resources efficiently and make informed decisions.

Core values

  • Innovation
  • Sustainability
  • Reliability
  • Accessibility
  • Integrity
  • Customer Focus

Problems addressed

The company addresses inefficient resource use, limited field monitoring, weak farm record-keeping and difficulty using agricultural information for timely decisions.

Target audience

Smallholder farmers, commercial farmers, cooperatives, agricultural estates, NGOs, irrigation schemes and agricultural development organisations in Malawi.

Brand identity

Built around agriculture and technology.

Logo symbolism

The green leaf-inspired form represents crop growth, food security and sustainability. The precision-inspired blue and yellow forms communicate innovation, data, irrigation and productivity.

Brand story

The identity blends natural agricultural cues with a modern technical aesthetic. The logo’s green tones signal plants and field vitality, while blue elements suggest digital systems, water management and precision agriculture. Yellow accents add warmth, energy and agricultural opportunity, creating a memorable, professional brand.

Reflection

What I learned from the project.

Through this project, I developed a deeper understanding of how multimedia tools can be used to build a credible and professional business identity. I improved my ability to combine visual communication, layout design, typography and branding strategy in a coherent manner.

I also gained practical knowledge about the importance of consistency, target audience awareness and presentation when communicating a business concept to stakeholders. The process made me more confident in creating work that is not only visually attractive, but also purposeful, professional and easy to understand.

  • Skills developed: logo design, layout composition, brand consistency and visual storytelling.
  • Knowledge gained: how agriculture-focused branding differs from general corporate branding and why professional presentation matters.
  • Challenges encountered: balancing a clean corporate look with enough agricultural symbolism to communicate the business clearly.
  • Brand consistency: maintaining the same logo, colours, typography and design language across every application.
  • Business communication: multimedia tools help businesses present themselves professionally and earn trust in the market.
